What we collect

Nothing. This site has no server, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Everything happens in your browser.

Files you open, such as a site boundary or a footprint, are read on your own device and are never uploaded.

Data your browser downloads is kept in your browser's own storage, so a second visit is faster. You can clear it at any time in your browser's settings.

Which other services your browser contacts

To fetch map data, your browser talks directly to some public services. They can see your IP address and the area or words you asked about, as any website you visit can. They are:

  • OpenStreetMap map tiles (tile.openstreetmap.org), for the background map.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), but only when you press Search. Nothing is sent as you type.
  • Overpass API (overpass-api.de and two mirrors), for buildings, roads, water, power and the other layers of an area you draw.
  • AWS Terrain Tiles (s3.amazonaws.com), for elevation of an area you draw.
  • EOX satellite tiles (tiles.maps.eox.at), but only if you choose a satellite surface in the preview.

Curated sites load from data bundled with this site, so choosing one contacts none of the services above, apart from the background map.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits as any web host does. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Site data

The curated site files record only corporate and planning information, with sources. They contain no information about residents, objectors or officials. Building layers show the type of building from OpenStreetMap, never names or addresses of homes.
